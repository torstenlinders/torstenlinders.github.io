Get involved

Kompass is a new political initiative. We are looking for people who would like to contribute ideas, knowledge and practical work.

For example, you could help with:

  • political ideas and fact-checking
  • writing and communication
  • organisation and administration
  • public outreach
  • practical work during the election campaign

You do not need previous experience in politics. What matters is that you share our ambition to contribute to a more optimistic political conversation in Sweden.

Register your interest

Send a short message to kontakt@kompass26.se and tell us:

  1. who you are
  2. what interests you
  3. how you would like to contribute

Kompass is still in an early stage. Information about membership and the party’s organisation will be published when decisions have been made.
