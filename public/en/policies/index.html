Policies

Kompass believes that Sweden is built every day by those of us who live and work here. The building blocks are dreams and work. We build together.

Growth, work and taxation

Economic growth matters, but longlivety and health matter even more.

It should pay to work, but we must share too.

We, not someone else, must pay taxes.

Every new challenge cannot be met by raising taxes.

Our shared challenges are growing: in healthcare, education, infrastructure and security. The scope for tax cuts is limited.

Taxes should be easy to understand, difficult to avoid and distort the economy as little as possible. This speaks for taxes on consumption and property. Income must also be taxed. Inheritance is income. The tax rates and exemptions should be few.

Planning and security

The future will always surprise us. Initiative, unity and determination therefore matter more than elaborate plans.

We build Sweden’s security together with our friends and allies, in the European Union and NATO.

The financial crisis of 2008, the pandemic, Russia’s aggressions, Trump’s threats against Greenland and NATO, and the rapid development of artificial intelligence, these are challenges we must meet together with our friends and allies.

Europe and immigration

Together with our neighbours and the rest of Europe, we are unstoppable.

Immigrants built yesterday’s Sweden, and without immigration today’s Sweden stagnates.

Non-Swedish citizens can play a larger role in Swedish politics and take up more seats in municipal and regional assemblies.

Non-Swedish citizens can contribute more to Sweden’s defence. The voluntary defence organisations and the Home Guard should offer clear paths for non-Swedish citizens.
