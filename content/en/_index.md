Kompass is an optimistic voice in Swedish politics, aiming at the 2026 election.

On this website, we present our ideas about Sweden today and in the future, and about how the political conversation in Sweden can become better. 
Kompass was started in August 2026 by oceanographer, Home Guard soldier, and father of three Torsten Linders.
